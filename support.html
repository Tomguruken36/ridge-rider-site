Common questions

How do I play?
Hold the right side of the screen for gas and the left side to brake. In the air, gas flips the truck backwards and brake flips it forwards. Land with both wheels together for a butter landing.
Why did my run end?
Landing on the roof wrecks the truck, and rolling to a stop with an empty battery ends the run. Flips, butter landings and the floating batteries refill it.
How do I unlock more trucks?
Reach 1,000 m in a single run for the Baja Blaster and 2,000 m for the Nitro Comet.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Use the speaker and vibration buttons above the truck cards on the start or wreck screen.
The game feels slow on my phone.
Ridge Rider lowers its graphics automatically on older iPhones after a few seconds of play, and remembers the setting.
Will I lose my records?
Records are saved on your device. They stay through updates, but deleting the app deletes them.
