Ridge Rider: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Ridge Rider does not collect any data about you.

What the game stores

Your best runs, unlocked trucks and settings (sound, vibration and graphics quality) are saved in a small file on your device so the game remembers them. This file never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

What the game does not do

No accounts or sign-in. No analytics, tracking or advertising. No connection to the internet during play. We don't collect, share or sell personal information, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before any new version that does so is released.
